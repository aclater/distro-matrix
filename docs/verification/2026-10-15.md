# Verification: 2026-10-15

A full check that the runner works end to end on the maintainer's laptop
(Framework, 24 cores, 62 GiB, Fedora 44), after the fixes for #43 (disks
smaller than the base image), #42 (`--prepare`), #39 (spawn-fleet on the
qemu backend) and #45 (Ctrl-C). Branch `fix/43-42-boot-prepare`, PR #44.

Scanners: pqc-readiness **2.3.0** as the scanner and **2.2.0** as the
compare build, both downloaded from the GitHub releases and checked
against their published `.sha256`. Images: the 18 in the local cache
(`~/.cache/pqc-vm-matrix/images`); the vm-run budget was 18 vCPUs (OpenShift
Local stopped).
